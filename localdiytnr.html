Please be aware that all TNR programs have waiting lists
SPRINGFIELD, MO

Call SAAF to schedule a TNR appointment at (417) 831-7223. These appointments are $40, and the cat must be in a trap and is required to get an ear tip. You can borrow a trap from SAAF for a $30 refundable deposit. SAAF also offers low-cost appointments for pets. View their website to learn more: https://www.spayneuterspringfield.org/services-pricing

Humane Society, I find the easiest way to make a low-cost appointment here is to visit them in person. There is also an online form at https://www.swh.org/spay-and-neuter--appointment-request-form.html Or you can try calling and leaving a message: (417) 833-2526 or email them at info@hsswmo.org

BRANSON

Branson Humane Society has launched a new Low-Cost spay and neuter clinic. For information and scheduling, go to: https://bransonhumanesociety.com/spay-and-neuter-clinic/ Note: You MUST pay in advance for this clinic, or they will cancel your appointment and turn you away

Shepard of the Hills offers low-cost services by calling 417-337-PETZ or booking an appointment online. https://practices.allydvm.com/online-booking?practice=shepherdofthehills

MARIONVILLE, MO

(Cats Only) Book online; they often have plenty of space for Males, but Females have a much longer waiting list. https://tinyurl.com/CASneuter

ROLLA, MO

The Carol House ( appointment ONLY) Book online or call 573-465-3099 to schedule or email: Reception.Rolla@QuickFixPetClinic.com

PINEVILLE, MO

Follow I’m Your Huckleberry Rescue - Spay/Neuter Clinic on Facebook for a low-cost appointment: tinyurl.com/IYHspayneuter

CASH ONLY
