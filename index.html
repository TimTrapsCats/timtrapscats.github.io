Tim Traps Cats

We currently have two active projects, Ozarks TNR and Stop Parvo Springfield Missouri.
Our Mission:
Tim Traps Cats works to create a healthier and more balanced urban ecosystem in Springfield, Missouri, by providing resources for spay, neuter, and vaccination services for unsheltered cat and dog populations.
